About ColoursFlux

A festival companion built for students on crowded campus grounds: fast, readable, and useful when the network is not.

What this app does

ColoursFlux brings the festival essentials into one place: the day-wise schedule, a campus map, stall and offer listings, and announcements from the organisers. It is designed for phones first, and it keeps working when the campus network gets busy.

  • Events — search by name, filter by day, category and venue, and save events to this device.
  • Campus map — stages, food court, technical venues, services and parking, with a searchable directory.
  • Stalls — vendor details, opening hours where provided, and time-limited offers.
  • Announcements — time changes, venue changes and urgent notices, newest first.

Festival information

How offline mode works

ColoursFlux is a progressive web app. When you load it once with a connection, the app shell is cached and the festival data is stored locally on your device. On later visits, the app opens from that cache and shows the saved schedule, map, stalls and notices.

  • First visit needs a connection. The app cannot install itself from nothing.
  • Tap “Download festival data” before you enter crowded areas to refresh the offline copy.
  • Offline data can go stale. New notices and last-minute changes need a connection; the app always shows when the data was last synced.
  • A QR code only opens the website. It does not make content available offline by itself.
  • Clearing your browser data removes the offline copy. You will need a connection to restore it.

Using the app

Open the site and save data

Use the “Download festival data” button on the home page while you still have signal.

Install it to your home screen

On Android use the browser menu → Install app. On iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Save the events you care about

Tap the bookmark icon. Saved events stay on this device and are available in the Saved filter.

Check announcements before you travel between venues

Timing and venue changes are published there first.

Vendor promotion

Basic stall listings are free for students. Organisers can optionally mark a vendor as featured or place a sponsored card; those placements are labelled in the interface. Illustrative demo pricing is ₹99 for a featured listing, ₹199 for a sponsored promotion, and ₹499+ for a sponsor package. These prices are examples, not confirmed rates, and this app does not process payments.

Frequently asked

Does the app work without internet?
Yes, after the first successful visit and a data download. The app shell and the festival data are stored on your device. Live changes still need a connection.
Why does it say my data may be out of date?
When the app is offline, or the last sync is old, it keeps showing your saved copy and says so. That is intentional — it is better to show saved times than invent new ones.
Do I need an account?
No. Browsing is public. Accounts exist only for authorised organisers to publish content from the admin dashboard.
How do I install it on my phone?
Android: browser menu → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”. iPhone (Safari): Share → “Add to Home Screen”. Full steps are on the installation page.
Are the offers guaranteed?
Offers are published by vendors and expire automatically in the app. Availability is decided by the vendor at the stall — the app does not process payments or issue vouchers.
Something is wrong with an event. Who do I tell?
Speak to the registration desk or the organiser contact published in this page. This app is a companion, not the official record of the festival.

Sample data notice

Unless the organiser has connected the database, this deployment shows clearly-labelled sample records for layout and testing. Sample records are never presented as live festival information.
