Privacy policy

A short, plain-language description of what this app stores and what it does not.

Last reviewed: at build time

Who runs this app

ColoursFlux is a festival companion for Colours 2K27 at Aditya University. It is operated by the festival organising team. Contact details, when published, appear on the About page.

What we collect from visitors

Nothing that identifies you. You do not need an account to browse. The app does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or third-party pixels by default.

  • Saved events and preferences are stored only in your browser (local storage).
  • Festival data is cached in your browser’s IndexedDB so the app works offline.
  • Optional browser notifications are generated locally by your browser if you enable them. No notification data is sent to us.

Organiser accounts

Authorised organisers sign in to a separate admin dashboard. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. We store the organiser’s email, display name and role, plus the content they publish. Admin sessions are kept in the browser that signed in and are never written into the public offline cache.

Hosting and service providers

  • Static hosting serves the website files.
  • Supabase provides the database, authentication and image storage for published festival content.

Those providers may process standard technical data (such as IP addresses and request logs) as part of delivering the service. Their own privacy terms apply to that processing.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell visitor data.
  • We do not process payments in this app. Vendor and sponsor promotions are managed manually by the organisers.
  • We do not claim to deliver notifications to devices that are offline.
  • We do not publish private organiser contact details or vendor private information.

Your controls

You can clear saved festival data at any time from the Install & offline page, or by clearing site data in your browser. Saved events can be removed individually with the bookmark button.

Children

This is a general-audience university festival app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the way this app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the review date above changed.

Contact

Privacy questions should go to the festival organising team. If no contact address is published yet, ask at the registration desk.

Note for the organising team: this policy describes the application as shipped. Review it, add your official contact details and confirm it matches your institution’s requirements before launch.
