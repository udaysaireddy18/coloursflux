Terms of use

The practical rules for using this festival companion.

1. What this app is

ColoursFlux is a student-built festival companion for Colours 2K27 at Aditya University. It helps visitors find events, venues, stalls and announcements. It is an information tool, not an official record, ticket, or emergency service.

2. Accuracy of information

Festival content is published by authorised organisers and can change at short notice. Cached and offline data may be out of date; the app shows when it was last synchronised. Always follow instructions from organisers and campus security on the ground.

3. Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to access the admin dashboard without authorisation.
  • Do not probe, scrape, overload or interfere with the service.
  • Do not upload unlawful, harmful or infringing content if you are given publishing access.
  • Do not use festival listings to harass vendors, performers or other visitors.

4. Offers and promotions

Stall offers are published by vendors and are subject to availability at the stall. Featured and sponsored placements are labelled. Demo pricing shown in the app is illustrative only. The app does not process payments and does not guarantee that an offer will be honoured.

5. Vendor and sponsor content

Vendors are responsible for the accuracy of their listings and for complying with campus rules. The organisers may reject, unpublish or expire a listing that breaches those rules.

6. Availability

The service is provided on a best-effort basis and may be unavailable during maintenance, heavy load, or network problems. Offline functionality depends on your browser and on the data having been cached earlier.

7. Intellectual property

8. Liability

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the organisers are not liable for losses arising from reliance on cached, delayed or incorrect festival information. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot be limited by law.

9. Changes

These terms may be updated as the festival approaches. The current version is always published on this page.
